      Welcome 👋

      Make a real circuit board in three steps — no electronics degree needed.

      1Describe it

      Type what you want in the Copilot box. “A blinking LED board powered by USB-C” is enough.

      2Look & test it

      Spin it in 3D, test power and heat, and check it fits your case. The AI already checked its own work.

      3Order it

      Press Export & order, pick a factory and upload the files. Boards arrive in about a week.

      Tip: you can always change the board by chatting — “make it smaller”, “add a button”, “make the PCB black”. Stuck? Press ? at the top.

      Help & glossary

      PCB
      Printed circuit board — the green (or any colour) board that holds and connects the parts.
      Layer
      PCBs are sandwiches of copper wiring. 2 layers is cheapest and fine for simple boards. 4+ layers dedicate inner layers to ground and power, which makes busy boards easier and quieter.
      Schematic
      The “circuit diagram”: symbols for each part and which pins connect.
      Net / connection
      A group of pins that are wired together, like “GND” or “3V3”.
      Design check (DRC)
      Automatic inspection that wires are not too close, nothing is short-circuited or left unconnected.
      Gerber
      The standard file format factories use to make the board. You upload a zip of them.
      BOM
      Bill of materials — the shopping list of parts. Part positions tell the factory’s robot where to place each one.
      Via
      A tiny plated hole that carries a wire from one layer to another.
      Regulator
      A part that turns a higher voltage (like USB’s 5 V) into a lower one (like 3.3 V). It gets warm — check it in Test power & heat.

      Shortcuts: Ctrl+Enter create · R rotate selected part · Delete remove it · arrow keys nudge it · double-click the board to fit the view.
